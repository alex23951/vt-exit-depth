## 2. D43 (adopted 13:13:21 UTC, "Adopt both"): the ECG at the infundibulum cut
Your ECG takes a merged node's Vm as the mean of its split copies. The ECG is linear in Vm, so the mean adds to the
per-copy ECG the sum over split nodes of (w1 - w2)(V2 - V1)/2, w1 and w2 each copy's weight on the split mesh. That is
a double layer of dipole density (sigma_i n)(V2 - V1) on the cut (n its normal, close to transmural, so about 0.062 S/m
control and 0.027 S/m HF times V2 - V1): wherever one wall is depolarised and the apposed wall is not, the ECG sees a
wavefront crossing a cut no current crosses, at most one of the cut's area, 140.1 / 135.5 / 154.7 mm2 (D32 (i); P12
(c)). D43 adopts this: every engine's ECG (12 leads and D36 (i)'s potentials) forms its source element by element on
the split mesh, each copy with its own Vm and its merged node's lead-field value; lead fields unchanged. Please change
the runner's 12 leads and electrode potentials to this before the first ionic beat; any ECG already formed from the
mean is corrected exactly from the kept split copies' 1 ms Vm before any use. Asks: which rule the graph leg's ECG
uses at the copies (if not this one, change it before any graph ECG is used); how many split nodes each heart has;
and, when D43 and D44 are logged, confirm that, apart from the window ranges in your 12:14 UTC checklist, no v5.2 ECG,
ECG measure, activation map or other activation value had been opened.
