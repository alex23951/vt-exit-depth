## What C73 rules
1. R3.1's cap at "the mesh's cut ends of the two great vessels" lies on each stub's rim: the open loop of edges where
   the cohort's luminal surface of the aorta (tag 5) or PA (tag 6) ends. The blood fills the whole lumen from the valve
   disc up to the rim; none is left out below it, none lies beyond it. Rules 1 and 2 of conductor52.py (861a760) are
   used for no v5.2 result. A cap across the lumen anywhere else, inside the stub or beyond its end, is not R3.1's and

2. **(2) How the rim is read.** The reference is rimcap.py (notes/A/2026-11-02-v52-lock/c73/rimcap.py, 13,954 B, sha256

   with no options on the cohort archive's myocardium_AV_FEC_BB_lvrv.bpts and .belem (their sizes and sha256 in
   c73/README.md). Its docstring states its rules and its code governs the surfaces; (3) governs which torso elements
   become blood. Per vessel (aorta: wall tag 5, disc tag 9, root tags 1, 25 and 27; PA: 6, 10, 2, 28 and 29), where the
   disc's and the wall's nodes are every node of a tetrahedron of that tag: (a) only boundary faces count, the faces of
   exactly one tetrahedron of the heart mesh, each oriented out of it; (b) o is the mean of the disc's nodes and a the
   unit eigenvector of their covariance with the smallest eigenvalue, signed towards the mean of the wall's nodes, with
   height h(x) = (x - o) . a; the ring coverage at a height z is the share of 36 sectors of 10 deg about the axis,
   counted from e1 = a x (1, 0, 0) normalised, that hold a wall node with |h - z| < 0.75 mm, and z_top is the last
   height of the unbroken run of 0.5 mm steps, each with coverage at least 0.9, that starts at the first of 1.0, 1.5,
   ..., 29.5 mm with coverage at least 0.9 (the run is not bounded by 29.5 mm: 6G8DD8S5's aortic z_top is 36.5 mm); (c)
   a wall face is luminal if its outward normal n points towards its axis point, n . (q - c) > 0 with c the face
   centroid and q = o + clamp(h(c), 0, z_top) a, so a face of the outer wall, which points away from the axis, never
   joins; a root-tag face joins if it passes the same test, has 0 < h(c) < h_root and has its centroid within rho_root
   of the axis, where h_root and rho_root are 1.0 mm above the highest node and 0.5 mm beyond the node farthest from the

   (e) the sheet's boundary edges, those whose face across is not in the sheet, are walked as loops by turning about
